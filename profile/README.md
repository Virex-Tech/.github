### What we do

We take an app from idea to the store, then run it. Design, backend, mobile, web, infrastructure, and the analytics that say whether it worked.

That last part changes how we write everything else. When you carry the pager for your own code, you stop shipping the kind that needs someone watching it.

<h3 align="center">How we build</h3>

<table align="center">
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**One language, whole stack**

Fastify and Prisma on the server, Next.js on the web, React Native on mobile. One set of types from the database row to the button.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**Domain-driven, not framework-driven**

Business rules live in the domain layer and stay testable without a database, a queue or a browser.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**Nothing starts from a blank page**

Auth, billing, push, observability and CI come pre-wired from our internal platform. A new product reaches production in days.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**Instrumented from day one**

Every product ships with the analytics already wired, so the first question after launch has an answer.

</td>
</tr>
</table>
